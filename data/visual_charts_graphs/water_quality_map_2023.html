At a glance:
• The darker the blue, the better the water quality; and only a few countries are dark blue.
• Water quality is measured through analysis of levels of chemicals such as pH or oxygen and levels of pollution.
• Why it matters: Good water quality is crucial for public health, environmental protection, and economic well-being. Clean water is essential for drinking, cooking, and sanitation, preventing the spread of waterborne diseases and other health issues. It supports healthy aquatic ecosystems, biodiversity, and recreational opportunities.
