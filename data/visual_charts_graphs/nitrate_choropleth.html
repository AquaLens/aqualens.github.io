At a glance:
• Global nitrogen levels rose sharply around the 1940s, likely reflecting post-war industrial and agricultural expansion.
• Although levels declined afterward, a gradual increase has resumed in recent years due to fertilizer use and land pressure.
• Continued monitoring is needed to prevent oxygen depletion, algae blooms, and long-term harm to aquatic ecosystems.
