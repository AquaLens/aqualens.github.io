At a glance:
• Countries are classified by water stress levels: green (low), yellow (medium), and red (high).
• Water stress is measured as the ratio of total freshwater withdrawn to renewable freshwater resources, accounting for environmental needs.
• Why it matters: High water stress indicates unsustainable water use and severe pressure of a country’s freshwater systems. The demand for water intensifies with the rapid growth of economies and population. As the detrimental effects of climate change become more frequent and extreme, this map highlights where urgent policy action is needed to prevent future water scarcity.
