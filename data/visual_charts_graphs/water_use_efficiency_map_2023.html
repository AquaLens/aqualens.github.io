At a glance:
• The map visualizes water use efficiency (US$/m³) by country for the year 2022. It represents how much economic value (in US dollars) is generated per cubic meter of water used, reflecting how effectively a country utilizes water to support its economy
• Why it matters: Efficient water usage is crucial for economic resilience, climate adaptation, and sustainable development. This indicator helps governments and stakeholders identify where water is being used productively, and where improvements are needed.
